Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026  |  Version: 2026-11

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Friendly mobile application ("Friendly", "we", "our", or "us") collects, how it is used and protected, and the choices and rights you have. Friendly is operated by Andrew Bates, doing business as Friendly.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide or that comes from your sign-in provider

  • Name and email address, from Apple Sign In, Google Sign-In, or email sign-in. Used for authentication, your profile, and account-related communication.
  • Username and account identifier, used to identify your account and enable search and connections.
  • Birthday. We ask for your birthday to confirm you meet our minimum age (13). It is stored in a private area of your account that only you can read. It is not shown to other users.
  • Profile information you choose to add: profile photo, bio, city, state, country, website, job title, company, and phone number.
  • Purchase information. If you subscribe to Friendly Pro, Apple processes the payment. We receive subscription status and transaction identifiers, not your payment card details.

Information about other people that you add

  • Contacts you add or import (names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, birthdays, notes, interactions, and reminders). See Section 4.

Information created when you use Friendly

  • Direct messages (content, sender, recipient, time, and reactions) between connected users.
  • Connections: who you follow, who follows you, and follow requests.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you submit about a user or message (including the reason and any details you write), and the list of accounts you have blocked.
  • Reminder and streak data used to power reminders, streaks, and relationship insights.
  • Device tokens used to deliver push notifications.
  • Cloud backup data, if you use the optional cloud backup feature (see Section 8).

Device permissions. Friendly asks for access to your contacts (to import people you choose), camera (to scan QR codes and take photos), photo library (to choose photos), and notifications. You can change these in your device settings at any time. Friendly works without them, with fewer features.

Diagnostics report. The Profile screen can generate a diagnostics report for troubleshooting. It contains your app version, device model, permission status, your account identifier, subscription status, the number of contacts, reminders, and interactions you have (not their contents), and recent app events and errors. It stays on your device and is shared with us only if you choose to send it.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To provide Friendly's features: contact management, reminders, relationship tracking, streaks, connections, and messaging.
  • To deliver direct messages and push notifications.
  • To verify that users meet our minimum age.
  • To provide optional cloud backup and restore, if you use it.
  • To process and verify Friendly Pro subscriptions.
  • To review reports, enforce our Community Rules and Terms, remove content, and suspend accounts that break them.
  • To respond to your support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing profiling, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK, and similar regions)

If the General Data Protection Regulation or similar law applies to you, we process your information: to perform our agreement with you (providing the app and your account); for our legitimate interests in keeping Friendly safe, preventing abuse, and enforcing our rules; to comply with legal obligations; and, where required, with your consent (for example, optional cloud backup, push notifications, and device permissions), which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Contact Information You Provide About Others

Friendly is a relationship management app, so it stores information about people in your life. This information comes from you, is used only to help you manage your own relationships, and is not shared with the contacts themselves, with other Friendly users, or with third parties. Your contact data is stored on your device. It is uploaded to our servers only if you turn on cloud backup. You are responsible for having a legitimate reason to store information about the people you add.

5. Direct Messages

Messaging is available only between mutually connected users. Messages are stored in Google Firebase so they can be delivered and so your conversation history is available. We do not read your messages, sell them, or use them for advertising. We may review message content only when it is reported to us, or when necessary to investigate abuse, protect safety, or comply with law. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

6. User Content, Reports, and Blocking

Friendly allows users to create profiles, set usernames, write bios, and send messages. You are responsible for what you submit, and it must follow our Community Rules.

  • Reporting. You can report a user or message by emailing our support address and, in versions of the app that include reporting, directly in the app. A report records who reported, who was reported, the reason, any details and related conversation or message identifiers, and the time. It is sent to us for review, including by email to our support address. We do not tell the reported person who reported them.
  • Blocking. In versions of the app that include blocking, you can block a user. Blocking ends your connection and conversation with them and prevents them from sending you new follow requests. We do not notify the blocked person. You can unblock from your settings, but unblocking does not automatically reconnect you.
  • Enforcement. We may remove content and suspend or terminate accounts that break our rules.

7. Discoverability

By default, your profile is not discoverable. If you turn on discoverability in your profile settings, other users can find you by username. You can turn it off at any time.

8. Data Storage and Cloud Backup

Your personal contact data is primarily stored on your device. The following is stored on Google Firebase to provide the app: authentication details; your profile information; your private birthday record; connection and follow data; direct messages; streak data; reports and blocks; subscription ownership records; and push notification tokens.

Cloud backup is optional. Cloud backup is a Friendly Pro feature. If you use it, your local app data (contacts and their phone numbers, email addresses, and addresses, plus notes, interactions, reminders, and settings) is uploaded to Firebase Storage so you can restore it. Only you can access your backup. Newer versions of the app let you choose whether to turn backup on or off in your Profile settings, and to delete your existing cloud backups; in those versions backup stays off until you turn it on. Deleting your account also deletes your cloud backups.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

  • We keep your data while your account is active.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete account). When you do, we delete your profile, username, profile photo, follow connections, streak data, blocked-account list, and cloud backups.
  • Messages: your messages in conversations are blanked and replaced with a "removed message" placeholder for the other person. If both people have deleted their accounts, the conversation is deleted.
  • Reports: reports you filed, or that name you, are kept for 12 months after we close them, for safety, moderation, and legal purposes, even after account deletion. We may keep a report longer when needed to comply with the law, to respond to a legal request, or to protect a child or another person from serious harm.
  • Backups and logs kept by our service providers may persist for a limited time before being removed by routine cycles.
  • Data stored only on your device can be deleted by removing the app.

10. Service Providers and Third-Party Services

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple; App Store purchases and subscriptions).
  • Google (Google Sign-In; Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, and Cloud Functions, used to run the app).
  • Resend (resend.com), used to email report notifications to our support address. Report notifications may include the report reason and any details you wrote.

These providers process data only to provide their services to us, under their own terms and privacy policies (for Google, see policies.google.com/privacy; for Apple, see apple.com/legal/privacy).

11. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only: with the service providers above; with other users as described in this policy (for example, your profile if you are discoverable, your messages with the people you message); when required by law, legal process, or a government request; to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or Friendly (including reporting content that exploits children to the authorities); and in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of the business, in which case we will tell you if your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

12. International Data Transfers

Friendly is operated from the United States, and our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using Friendly you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in countries whose data protection laws differ from those where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as our providers' standard contractual clauses.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, account-based access controls, and security rules that limit who can read or write data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you as required by law.

14. Children's Privacy

Friendly is not intended for anyone under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. We ask for your birthday as part of account setup. If you indicate you are under 13, we do not create an account, and the app may remember this on that device only. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Your Rights and Choices

  • In the app: edit your profile, turn discoverability on or off, manage cloud backup (including turning it off and deleting backups, in versions that include these controls), block users (in versions that include blocking), manage notifications and device permissions, and delete your account.
  • All users: you may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us below.
  • EEA/UK residents: you may also have rights to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California residents: you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request deletion and correction; and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law. You may use an authorized agent to make a request.

We will respond to verified requests within the time required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity first.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new effective date. For material changes, we will notify you in the app and ask you to review and accept the updated policy.
