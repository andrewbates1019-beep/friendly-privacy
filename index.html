Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026  |  Version: 2026-11-22

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Friendly mobile application ("Friendly", "we", "our", or "us") collects, how it is used and protected, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have. Friendly is owned and run personally by Andrew Bates, doing business as Friendly. Andrew Bates is responsible for your information (the "controller" or "business" under privacy laws).

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide or that comes from your sign-in provider

  • Sign-in details. Your email address and the sign-in method you use (Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or email and password). These are held by Firebase Authentication. If you use email sign-in, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Your Friendly profile: first and last name, display name, username, bio, profile photo, whether you are discoverable, and whether you send read receipts. This is stored in the cloud so the people you connect with can see it.
  • Extra profile details you choose to add on the profile screen (such as phone number, city, state, country, website, job title and company) are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded and are not shown to other users.
  • Birthday. We ask for your birthday to confirm you meet our minimum age (13). It is stored in a private area of your account that only you can read, so you are not asked again after reinstalling. It is never shown to other users. We also record the date you passed the age check.
  • Legal acceptance. The date you agreed to our Terms of Use, Community Rules and this Privacy Policy, and which version you agreed to.
  • Purchase information. If you subscribe to Friendly Pro, Apple processes the payment. We receive your subscription status, the product you bought and Apple's transaction identifiers. We do not receive your payment card details.

Information about other people that you add

  • Contacts you add or import (names, phone numbers, email addresses, physical addresses, birthdays, social handles, tags, notes, interactions and reminders). This stays on your device unless you turn on cloud backup. See Section 4.

Information created when you use Friendly

  • Direct messages between connected users: the text, who sent it, the time, reactions, replies, whether it was edited or unsent, and whether and when it was read.
  • Connections: who you follow, who follows you, and follow requests.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you submit about a user or message (including the reason and any details you write), and the list of accounts you have blocked.
  • Streak data used to power weekly streaks and streak reminders.
  • Device tokens used to deliver push notifications, one for each device you are signed in on.
  • Cloud backup data, only if you turn on cloud backup (see Section 8).

Device permissions. Friendly asks for access to your contacts (to import people you choose), camera (to scan QR codes and take photos), photo library (to choose photos), and notifications. You can change these in your device settings at any time. Friendly works without them, with fewer features.

Diagnostics in feedback emails. When you tap "Send feedback" or "Report a bug" in the app, Friendly opens an email to us that includes a diagnostics report: your app version, device model, permission status, your account identifier, subscription status, how many contacts, reminders and interactions you have (not what they contain), and recent app events and errors. You can read or remove it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Data collected by Google's software in the app. Friendly uses Google's Firebase and Google Sign-In software to run the app. That software automatically collects some technical information to deliver, secure and measure the performance of Google's own services: device and installation identifiers, diagnostic information (for example, errors and how long requests take), and, when you sign in with Google, information about your sign-in session that may include your approximate location (for example, from your IP address). Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. We do not use it ourselves for analytics, advertising or profiling.

We do not add advertising or analytics tools of our own, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To provide Friendly's features: contact management, reminders, relationship tracking, streaks, connections and messaging.
  • To deliver direct messages and push notifications.
  • To check that users meet our minimum age, and to keep accounts under the minimum age from being found in search.
  • To provide optional cloud backup and restore, if you turn it on.
  • To process and verify Friendly Pro subscriptions, and to stop one subscription from being claimed by more than one account.
  • To review reports, enforce our Community Rules and Terms of Use, remove content, and suspend accounts that break them.
  • To respond to your support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising or marketing profiling, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK and Similar Regions)

If the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR or a similar law applies to you, we process your information: to perform our agreement with you (providing the app and your account); for our legitimate interests in keeping Friendly safe, preventing abuse and enforcing our rules; to comply with legal obligations; and, where required, with your consent (for example, optional cloud backup, push notifications and device permissions), which you can withdraw at any time.

4. Contact Information You Provide About Others

Friendly is a relationship management app, so it stores information about people in your life. This information comes from you, is used only to help you manage your own relationships, and is not shared with the contacts themselves, with other Friendly users or with third parties. Your contact data is stored on your device. It is uploaded to our servers only if you turn on cloud backup. You are responsible for having a legitimate reason to store information about the people you add.

5. Direct Messages

Messaging is available only between connected users. Messages are stored in Google Firebase so they can be delivered and so your conversation history is available on any device you sign in on. We do not read your messages, sell them or use them for advertising. We may look at message content only when it is reported to us, or when necessary to investigate abuse, protect someone's safety or comply with the law. Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest by our provider, but they are not end-to-end encrypted.

You can edit or unsend a message within 2 minutes of sending it. Unsending removes its text and reactions and leaves a placeholder. If you stop following each other, you can no longer send new messages, but the conversation history stays visible to both of you.

6. User Content, Reports and Blocking

Friendly lets users create profiles, set usernames, write bios and send messages. You are responsible for what you submit, and it must follow our Community Rules.

  • Reporting. You can report a user or message in the app or by emailing our support address. A report records who reported, who was reported, the reason, any details you write (up to 500 characters), any related conversation or message identifiers, and the time. Only we can read reports. We are also notified by email (see Section 11). We do not tell the reported person who reported them.
  • Blocking. When you block someone, we remove the follow connection between you in both directions, they cannot send you new follow requests, and neither of you can send new messages. Your earlier conversation history is not deleted. We do not tell the blocked person. You can unblock from your settings, but unblocking does not reconnect you.
  • Enforcement. We may remove content and suspend or close accounts that break our rules.

7. Discoverability

By default, your profile is not discoverable. If you turn on discoverability, other users can find you by username. You can turn it off at any time. Discoverability can only be turned on when a birthday showing you meet the minimum age is on file.

Whether or not you are discoverable, people you follow, who follow you, who have a pending follow request with you, or who have a conversation with you can see your Friendly profile (name, username, bio and photo).

8. Data Storage and Cloud Backup

Most of your data (your contacts, notes, interactions, reminders, settings and extra profile details) is stored only on your device. The following is stored in Google Firebase to run the app: your sign-in record; your Friendly profile and profile photo; your private birthday record; connection and follow data; direct messages; streak data; reports and blocks; subscription ownership records; and push notification tokens.

Cloud backup is optional and is a Friendly Pro feature. It stays off until you turn it on in Profile > Settings. While it is on, Friendly uploads a copy of your on-device data (your contacts and their details, notes, interactions, reminders and app settings, including the name, email and phone number in your settings) to Firebase Storage, at most about once a day, so you can restore it. Only you can access your backup. When you turn backup off, you can choose to keep or delete the backups already in the cloud.

Older app versions. In versions of Friendly before 2.2, cloud backup turned on automatically for Friendly Pro subscribers. If you are still using one of those versions, backups continue automatically until you update. After you update, automatic backups pause and the app asks whether you want backup on or off; backup stays off unless you choose to turn it on. Backups already saved remain until you delete them or delete your account.

10. What Deleting Your Account Removes

You can delete your account in the app at Profile > Settings > Advanced > Delete account, or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent. When you delete your account:

We delete:

  • Your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke Friendly's access to your Apple ID.
  • Your Friendly profile (name, display name, bio, discoverability and read-receipt settings, legal acceptance record and age-check record).
  • Your private birthday record.
  • Your username, so it is no longer linked to you.
  • Your profile photo.
  • Your push notification tokens (they are stored on your profile and deleted with it).
  • Your follow connections and follow requests, in both directions.
  • Your blocked-accounts list, and any blocks other people made that name you.
  • Your streak data.
  • Your cloud backups.
  • The Friendly data on the device you delete from.

Messages: the text and reactions of messages you sent are erased and the other person sees a "removed message" placeholder. Your reactions to their messages, and any quote of your text in their replies, are also removed. The message records themselves (time sent and read status) stay in the conversation until the other person also deletes their account, at which point the whole conversation is deleted.

We keep:

  • Reports you filed or that name you, as described in Section 9.
  • The subscription ownership record, as described in Section 9. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings.
  • Copies in our providers' backups and logs until their routine cycles remove them.
  • Anything we must keep to comply with the law.

What we cannot remove: information other users stored on their own devices, such as a contact card they made for you, notes they wrote, or a copy of your name, username or photo link saved when you connected.

If our age check shows an account belongs to someone under 13, the app deletes the account in the same way.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, encryption at rest by our providers, account-based access controls, and security rules that limit who can read or write data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we learn of a security breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you, and any regulator we are required to notify, without unreasonable delay and within the time required by applicable law, by email or in the app. The notice will explain what happened, what information was involved, what we are doing, and what you can do.

15. Children's Privacy

Friendly is not intended for anyone under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. We ask for your birthday before an account is created. If you indicate you are under 13, we do not create an account and the app remembers this on that device only, without storing the birthday. If an existing account's birthday shows the person is under 13, the app deletes the account and its data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

  • Elsewhere: we will respond within the time required by the law that applies to you, and otherwise within 45 days.

Appeals. If we decline your request, in whole or in part, we will explain why. You can appeal by replying to our answer, or emailing us, with "Privacy appeal" in the subject within 60 days. We will review the decision and tell you the result, with our reasons, within 45 days. If you are not satisfied with the result, you can contact your state attorney general or, in the EEA or UK, your data protection authority.
