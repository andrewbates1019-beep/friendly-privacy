Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Friendly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Friendly mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Friendly:

  • Name: your name, provided during sign-in via Apple Sign In or Google Sign-In, and displayed on your profile
  • Email Address: collected during sign-in via Apple Sign In or Google Sign-In, used for authentication and account-related communication
  • User ID: a unique username you create, along with an internal account identifier, used to identify your account and enable features such as search and connections
  • Photos or Videos: a profile photo, either provided through your sign-in provider or uploaded by you
  • Contacts: information about the personal contacts you add or import into the app, used to power Friendly's relationship management features
  • Phone Number: phone numbers you add for your personal contacts within the app
  • Physical Address: physical addresses you add for your personal contacts within the app
  • Emails or Text Messages: the content, sender, and recipient of direct messages sent between mutually connected Friendly users

We also collect basic usage data to support core app functionality (such as reminders and relationship insights) and device tokens used to deliver push notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to provide and improve the Friendly app experience. Specifically:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your account
  • To enable core app features including contact management, reminders, and relationship tracking
  • To enable the Connections feature, allowing you to find and follow other Friendly users
  • To deliver direct messages between mutually connected users
  • To send push notifications for direct messages and reminders
  • To provide optional cloud backup and restore functionality

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the features described above.

3. Contact Information You Provide About Others

Friendly is a relationship management app, and its core function involves storing information about people in your life — your personal contacts. This may include their name, phone number, email address, and physical address, along with any notes, interactions, or reminders you create. This information is provided directly by you and is used solely to help you maintain and track your personal relationships. It is not shared with the contacts themselves, with other Friendly users, or with any third party.

4. Direct Messages

Friendly includes an in-app direct messaging feature available to mutually connected users. Message content, along with sender and recipient information, is stored securely in Firebase and is used solely to deliver messages between participants. Message content is never read, analyzed, sold, or shared with any third party. Messages are linked to your authenticated user account.

5. User-Generated Content

Friendly allows users to create profiles, set usernames, write bios, and send direct messages. This content is stored securely and is only visible to other users as permitted by the app's privacy and connection settings. Users are responsible for the content they submit. We reserve the right to remove content that violates our Terms of Service.

6. Discoverability

By default, your profile is not discoverable by other Friendly users. You may optionally enable discoverability in your profile settings, which allows other users to find you by username. You can turn this off at any time.

7. Data Storage

Your personal contact data is primarily stored locally on your device.

The following data is stored in Firebase (Google) as required to provide core app functionality:

  • Authentication credentials
  • Your public profile information (username, display name, bio, profile photo)
  • Connection and follow relationship data
  • Direct message content
  • Push notification device tokens

Friendly also offers an optional cloud backup feature. If enabled, your local app data — including contacts (with any associated phone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses), notes, interactions, reminders, and settings — may be securely uploaded to Firebase for backup and restore purposes. Cloud backup is entirely optional and is only performed with your explicit action.

8. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Direct message history is retained to provide continuity of your conversations within the app. If you delete your account, your data will be removed from our systems within a reasonable period. Local data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

9. Third-Party Services

Friendly uses the following third-party services to provide its functionality:

  • Apple Sign In / Google Sign-In: for authentication
  • Firebase (Google): for authentication, profile storage, connection data, direct messaging, push notifications, and optional cloud backup

These services may process limited data as required to provide their functionality. We do not control their privacy practices. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not share your personal data with third parties except when required to provide core app functionality such as authentication, messaging, or optional cloud backup.

Any data stored with third-party services is used solely to provide the functionality you request and is not used for advertising or tracking.

11. Children's Privacy

Friendly is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
