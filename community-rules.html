Community Rules

Effective date: November 22, 2026  |  Version: 2026-11-22

Friendly is for staying close to the people you know. These rules keep it safe and respectful. When you create an account you agree to follow them. They apply to everything you do in Friendly, including your profile, username, bio, photos, and direct messages.

1. Who can use Friendly

You must be 13 or older.

2. What is not allowed

Do not use Friendly to:

  • Harass, bully, or threaten anyone, or send content meant to intimidate or humiliate.
  • Post or send hateful content that attacks people because of who they are, such as race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
  • Send sexually explicit content, or sexual content involving anyone without their consent.
  • Exploit or endanger children. We have zero tolerance for content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors. We remove it, close the account, and report it to the authorities as the law requires.
  • Encourage violence, self-harm, or other dangerous acts.
  • Impersonate another person, or pretend to be someone you are not.
  • Spam, scam, or mislead people, including sending unwanted bulk messages or links.
  • Share other people's private information, such as phone numbers, addresses, or messages, without their permission.
  • Break the law, or use Friendly for anything illegal.

3. Respect people's choices

  • People can stop following you, remove you, or block you at any time, for any reason, and they are not required to tell you.
  • If someone does this, do not try to contact them afterward through Friendly, and do not make another account to get around it.
  • Do not try to get around a suspension, removal, or block.

4. The information you keep about other people

Friendly lets you store information about people in your life. Only store what you have a legitimate reason to keep, and do not use Friendly to track, stalk, or gather information about anyone against their wishes.

5. Reporting a person or message

  • In the app: report a message (press and hold it), a conversation (from its menu), or a person (from their profile). Choose a reason and add details if you like. You can also block the person at the same time.
  • By email: andrewbates.friendly.dev@gmail.com
  • Reports are kept confidential. We do not tell the person you reported who made the report.
  • Please report in good faith. Knowingly false or abusive reports break these rules too.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. We review reports, but we do not monitor Friendly in real time.

6. Blocking

You can block anyone from their profile or a conversation. Blocking ends your connection and conversation with them, and they cannot send you new follow requests. We do not tell them they were blocked. You can unblock someone in your settings, but unblocking does not automatically reconnect you.

7. What we do about violations

We review reports as soon as we reasonably can. We do not read private messages unless they are reported to us or we need to investigate abuse, protect safety, or comply with the law. If content or an account breaks these rules, we may:

  • remove the content;
  • warn the person, restrict what they can do, or suspend or permanently close their account; and
  • report illegal activity to the authorities.

The action we take depends on how serious the violation is and whether it has happened before. We may act without notice when needed to protect people. If you think we made a mistake, email us and we will look again.

8. Copyright and takedowns

  • Only share photos, text and other content that you created or have permission to use.
  • If you believe something on Friendly infringes your copyright, send us a takedown notice as described on our Copyright and DMCA page. If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can send a counter-notice the same way.
  • We remove content when we receive a valid notice, and we close the accounts of people who repeatedly infringe.

9. Changes

We may update these rules. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and ask you to review it in the app.
